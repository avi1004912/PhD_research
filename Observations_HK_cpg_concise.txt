Pure HK works for simple quadrupeds (2–4 joints, lighter legs).
Ant is too complex for purely emergent HK walking..


HK+CPG+torso_stabilisation:

Hybrid HK + Gait Approach (No RL) + torso_stabilisation.
Minimal CPG + Swing/Stance Enforcement.
High dimensionality problem.
When one leg lifts too far, HK reaches a new local minimum (tripod stance), and the system freezes.
We can’t increase the amplitude much without destabilizing the torso..
If we increase the CPG amplitude too much, the robot loses balance and falls.
Pure HK + minimal CPG works on simple quadrupeds (2–4 joints per leg, lighter bodies)..
Ant is too heavy, too coupled, and too high-DoF for sustained pure HK locomotion..


Approach 3 : Explicit fully scripted trajectory reinforcement:

Precompute hip/knee angles over time for each leg.
Use of predefined joint trajectories for swing and stance.
Even with a “scripted trot” + HK, the Ant still does not walk.
Homeokinesis alone cannot generate global gait timing, only local coordination.
HK adjusts joint gains and biases to reduce prediction error..
